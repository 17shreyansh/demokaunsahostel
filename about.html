About KaunsaHostel

We're on a mission to provide safe, comfortable, and affordable accommodation for students and professionals in Greater Noida.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, KaunsaHostel emerged from a simple observation: finding quality accommodation in Greater Noida was unnecessarily complicated and stressful for students and young professionals.

Our founders, having experienced the challenges of hostel hunting firsthand, decided to create a platform that would simplify this process while ensuring high standards of safety, comfort, and affordability.

Today, we're proud to have helped thousands of students and professionals find their perfect home away from home in Greater Noida.

Our Mission

To revolutionize the hostel experience by providing safe, comfortable, and affordable accommodation options that feel like home, enabling students and professionals to focus on their goals.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and preferred accommodation platform in India, setting new standards for student and professional housing across the country.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Safety First
