Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I book a hostel?

You can book a hostel by browsing our listings, selecting your preferred accommodation, and filling out the booking form. Our team will contact you within 24 hours to confirm your booking.

What documents are required for booking?

You'll need a valid ID proof (Aadhar Card, Passport, or Driver's License), passport-size photographs, and proof of enrollment/employment. Additional documents may be required based on the specific hostel's policies.

Is there a security deposit?

Yes, most hostels require a refundable security deposit equivalent to one month's rent. This deposit is returned when you vacate the room, subject to any deductions for damages.

Can I visit the hostel before booking?

Absolutely! We encourage prospective residents to visit and inspect the facilities before making a booking. Contact us to schedule a visit at your convenience.
